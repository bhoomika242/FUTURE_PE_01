# Business Context – Celira Fashion & Lifestyle Brand

## Business Name
Celira

## Industry
Fashion and Lifestyle

## Business Description

Celira is a modern fashion and lifestyle brand that offers trendy western wear, footwear, bags, and fashion accessories for men and women aged 15 and above.

The brand focuses on providing stylish, luxury-inspired, and affordable fashion for customers who want to express their personality through clothing and accessories.
